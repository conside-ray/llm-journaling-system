### Example entry: Ambiguous tasks create avoidance
- Type: execution pattern
- Observation: I tend to procrastinate more on tasks that are ambiguous than on tasks that are difficult but clearly defined.
- Why it matters: This affects execution quality across business and personal systems work.
- Evidence: Repeated in multiple weekly reviews and daily shutdowns.
- Implication / action: Define the first visible step before starting work on any ambiguous task.
